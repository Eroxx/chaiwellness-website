Chai, the word and the drink.

חי

Life

Chai is the Hebrew word for life. It is what a wellness company is ultimately about.

18

A number with a blessing

The two letters of chai add up to 18, which is why gifts and donations are so often given in multiples of it. L'chaim means "to life."
