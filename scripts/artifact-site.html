About

Emily R. Mouilso, PhD, ABPP

Board-certified clinical psychologist. She helps therapists and clinics do the hard parts of the work well: treating trauma, supervising carefully and making sound ethical decisions.

Dr. Emily Mouilso

Dr. Emily Mouilso is a licensed psychologist in Georgia and is board certified in Behavioral and Cognitive Psychology by the American Board of Professional Psychology. She is a Clinical Associate Professor at the University of Georgia, where she directs the Psychology Clinic and serves as Associate Director of Clinical Training for the doctoral program in clinical psychology.

Her clinical roots are in trauma. She trained at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center and the National Crime Victims Research and Treatment Center in Charleston, then completed a fellowship at the Charlie Norwood VA Medical Center, where she treated veterans and set up a new co-located psychology clinic on a unit that had never had embedded behavioral health care.

Since 2015 she has run a training clinic, which means she spends her days on the questions clinicians actually face: which treatment fits, how to supervise it, and what the right thing to do is when the answer is not obvious. When her clinic moved to telehealth in 2020 she chose the secure technology and wrote the consent and crisis procedures. She later led her program's policies on telesupervision and on artificial intelligence, and other clinics at her university have adopted the AI policy.

She has served on the Georgia Psychological Association's Ethics Committee since 2021, advising colleagues on difficult dilemmas, and is an exam chair for her specialty board at ABPP and a site visitor for the American Psychological Association's accreditation body.

She runs Chai Wellness with her husband, Eric Linder, who builds its apps.

Consulting

Training, consultation and ethics

Practical, evidence-based help for the people who do clinical work and the organizations that support them. In person or virtual, for a single session or an ongoing series.

Current offerings

For clinicians, clinics and organizations

01

Evidence-based treatment training

Skills training that clinicians can use the next morning, delivered with the supervision and implementation support that make it stick.

  • Cognitive behavioral therapy foundations
  • Exposure therapy and trauma-focused treatment
  • The Unified Protocol for emotional disorders
  • Implementing manualized, empirically supported treatments
02

Suicide prevention and risk work

Training in structured, collaborative approaches to assessing and treating suicidal thoughts, for clinicians and the people who supervise them.

  • Collaborative Assessment and Management of Suicidality
  • Cognitive behavioral therapy for suicide prevention
03

Clinical ethics

Ethics consultation and continuing education grounded in the APA Ethics Code and the realities of current practice. Case-based, candid and useful.

  • Ethical decision making through case analysis
  • Technology, social media and telepsychology
  • Artificial intelligence in mental health care
  • Mandated reporting and practice after Roe
04

Supervision, training and program development

For training clinics, practices and agencies that want to supervise well, build policy and grow their people.

  • Supervision of CBT and exposure therapy
  • Telesupervision and AI policies
  • Setting up and running a training clinic
  • Integrated primary care and interdisciplinary partnerships
  • Preparing for board certification

Good to know

What this is, and isn't

Consulting through Chai Wellness is education, training and professional consultation for clinicians and organizations. It is not therapy, and it does not provide crisis services. If you or someone you know is in crisis, call or text 988 (the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline) or your local emergency number.
